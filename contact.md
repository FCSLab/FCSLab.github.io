## Join the lab
<br>
New students and early-career researchers are admitted as opportunities and resources allow.
<br>
Students and postdocs interested in joining the lab should email Kimberly.Yazzie@ubc.ca with the subject line:
<br>
"Inquiring about FCS lab openings for [September/January/June] in [Year]."
<br><br>
Please include:
<br>a) a half-page cover letter outlining your research project ideas
<br>b) your CV
<br>c) contact information for two references
<br><br>Feel free to include funding programs you're considering applying to.

More information on program requirements and funding opportunities is available at the links below. 
<br><br>

## Collaborate with us
<br>
We welcome collaborative partnerships with knowledge holders, organizations, and institutions across sectors. If you're interested in working together, whether on a research project, conference panel, community-engaged initiative, or another form of collaboration, please reach out to Kimberly.Yazzie@ubc.ca with a subject line that clearly indicates the nature of your interest (e.g., "Partnership Inquiry: Community Research Project").
<br><br>
## Inquiries
<br>
Please send an email to Dr. Kimberly Yazzie at Kimberly.Yazzie@ubc.ca
